## Purpose

Transform campaign inputs into a normalized Campaign Contract.

Campaign Definition preserves:

- business objectives
- key messages
- offers
- audience definitions
- channel requirements
- campaign constraints
- mandatory requirements

The objective is not strategic reasoning.

The objective is creating a reliable campaign contract for downstream systems.

---

# Decision Architecture

Each layer owns a specific decision type.

A layer may influence downstream decisions.

A layer must not directly make downstream decisions.

| Layer                   | Decision Type            |
|-------------------------|--------------------------|
| Campaign Brief          | Campaign Definition      |
| Brand Intelligence      | Truth Discovery          |
| Strategy                | Meaning Selection        |
| Narrative               | Emotional Interpretation |
| Art Direction           | Visual Interpretation    |
| Scene Assembly          | Scene Construction       |
| Composition & Rendering | Visual Expression        |
| Synthesis               | Communication Resolution |
| Prompt Compiler         | Prompt Translation       |
| Image Generator         | Asset Execution          |

Current Layer:

Campaign Definition

Determine the campaign requirements that must remain true throughout the creative pipeline.

# Campaign Information Extraction

Extract and normalize:

- Business Objective
- Key Message
- Offer
- Audience
- Channel
- Campaign Constraints
- Mandatory Requirements

Remove:

- research notes
- meeting notes
- strategic reasoning
- creative recommendations
- implementation ideas
- execution suggestions

Only information required by downstream systems should remain.

# Boundaries & Guardrails

Campaign Brief owns:

- Business Objective
- Key Message
- Offer
- Audience Definition
- Channel Definition
- Campaign Constraints
- Mandatory Requirements

Campaign Brief does NOT own:

- Strategic Direction
- Behavioral Positioning
- Identity Migration
- Narrative Design
- Visual Concepts
- Scene Construction
- Visual Expression
- Generation Execution

---

# Campaign Validation Register

All assertions must resolve to TRUE.

ObjectiveDefined:
BusinessObjective exists.

MessageDefined:
PrimaryMessage exists.

AudienceDefined:
PrimaryAudience exists.

ChannelDefined:
PrimaryChannel exists.

ConstraintsDefined:
CampaignConstraints exist.

BoundaryProtected:
Campaign Contract contains no:
- strategic reasoning
- behavioral positioning
- identity migration
- narrative decisions
- visual decisions
- execution instructions

Any FALSE assertion triggers contract revision.
