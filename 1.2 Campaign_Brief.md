## Purpose

Transform campaign inputs into a normalized Campaign Contract.

Campaign Definition preserves:

- business objectives
- key messages
- offers
- audience definitions
- channel requirements
- campaign constraints
- mandatory requirements
- a named product model, when the campaign requires one to appear in generated imagery

The objective is not strategic reasoning.

The objective is creating a reliable campaign contract for downstream systems.

> **Note:** This is the canonical version of Campaign Brief. It is a strict superset of `1.` and `1.1` — when no specific product model is required, `ProductSpec` is simply omitted and this behaves exactly like `1. Campaign_Brief.md`. `1.` and `1.1` are kept in the repo as historical/reference only.

# Product Spec

Purpose:

Capture both the product identifier and its key visual descriptors when a campaign requires a specific product model to appear in generated imagery.

**Why visual descriptors and not just the model name:** Image generators may not have reliable training data for a specific product model. The model name alone produces generic results. The `key_visual_identifiers` are the actual visual instruction — the name is the label that anchors it.

**Include ProductSpec when:**
- The campaign brief names a specific product model (e.g. "feature the Pegasus 41")
- A specific product colorway is mandated
- The product's design is distinctive enough that a generic version would be incorrect

**Omit ProductSpec when:**
- No specific product model is required — a generic version of the product category is acceptable
- The campaign is brand-level, not product-level

**Multiple products:**
ProductSpec is an array. If the campaign features multiple named products, add one entry per product.

**Downstream flow:**
ProductSpec entries flow to Scene Assembly (Stage 5) as named entities with Near Exact preservation priority. Scene Assembly must generate a PreservationContract per product that includes the model name AND visual identifiers. Both must reach the Prompt Compiler's `brand` key in HIGH.

# Boundaries & Guardrails

Campaign Brief owns:

- Business Objective
- Key Message
- Offer
- Audience Definition
- Channel Definition
- Campaign Constraints
- Mandatory Requirements
- Product Spec (when applicable)

Campaign Brief does NOT own:

- Strategic Direction
- Behavioral Positioning
- Identity Migration
- Narrative Design
- Visual Concepts
- Scene Construction
- Visual Expression
- Generation Execution

# Campaign Validation Register

All assertions must resolve to TRUE.

ObjectiveDefined:
BusinessObjective exists.

MessageDefined:
PrimaryMessage exists.

AudienceDefined:
PrimaryAudience exists.

ChannelDefined:
PrimaryChannel exists.

ConstraintsDefined:
CampaignConstraints exist.

ProductSpecConsistent:
If a campaign brief names a specific product model, ProductSpec exists and includes model name, visual identifiers, and visibility requirement. Colorway is included only if mandated — not inferred or assumed.

BoundaryProtected:
Campaign Contract contains no:
- strategic reasoning
- behavioral positioning
- identity migration
- narrative decisions
- visual decisions
- execution instructions

Any FALSE assertion triggers contract revision.
