## Visual Prompt

```
VisualPrompt: "Candid lifestyle photograph of two young Asian professionals in their late twenties, standing on an indoor pickleball court, sharing a genuine laugh between rallies. Both holding pickleball paddles loosely at their sides. Eye-level medium shot. Subjects in sharp focus, filling the foreground — faces clearly readable, expressions natural and unposed, mid-laugh or mid-conversation. Athletic casual wear — modern, clean, non-uniform. GigaSports logo visible on a banner or wall in the warm background, soft in bokeh but legible. Pickleball net and court line markings visible in midground. Indoor sports venue with warm overhead ambient lighting — bright, social, community-oriented atmosphere. Shallow depth of field separates subjects from background. Warm color temperature. No studio lighting, no dramatic contrast. Real venue imperfections — natural ceiling, used court surface. Realistic lifestyle editorial photography quality. 4:5 vertical format. Clear empty space in bottom 20% of frame for text overlay."
```

## Prompt Notes for Image Generator

- **Platform:** Optimized for Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Flux (realistic lifestyle photography mode)
- **Aspect ratio flag:** Add `--ar 4:5` in Midjourney
- **Style modifier (Midjourney):** `--style raw` or `--style 250` recommended to reduce over-stylization and preserve candid realism
- **If brand element renders poorly:** Run a second pass with inpainting to add GigaSports logo to background banner area
- **Safe zone:** Bottom 20% of frame will receive Cantonese copy overlay and membership CTA — ensure no critical subject elements fall in this region
