# Scene Contract
**Brand:** GigaSports
**Campaign:** Gigasports Pickle Club Community
**Framework:** Scene Assembly Framework v5.0
**Stage:** 5 — Scene Construction

---

## Inputs

**Global Pipeline State:**
- Aspect Ratio: 4:5 (Instagram feed post)
- Target Resolution: 1080×1350
- Safe Zones: Top 10% and bottom 20% reserved for text overlay and CTA

**From Art Direction:**
- Visual Intent: Belonging + Human Warmth + Ease
- Visual Concept: Social Encouragement in Motion — candid between-play social moment on pickleball court
- Subject Relationship Logic: Equality and Partnership — viewer as potential peer, not aspirational observer
- Perceptual Meaning: "This feels socially welcoming. These are people like me. I could belong here."
- Focal Priority: Human faces and social interaction first; sport context second

**Uploaded Assets:** None — full generation required

No uploaded assets provided. All entities must be generated.

Generation should be informed by:
- GigaSports brand context: multi-sport retailer, Hong Kong market, community-and-event-association brand behavior
- Audience profile: young HK professionals aged 25–40, urban, middle-to-high income, athletic casual wear
- Campaign context: Pickleball as social activity — community club setting, not competitive venue

## Preservation Contracts

All entities are generated, so preservation contracts define generation requirements rather than asset fidelity constraints.

### entity_01 — Subject Group

**Preservation Priority:** Critical

**Recognizability Requirement:** Strong Match (consistency of social dynamic must survive rendering)

**Immutable Attributes:**
- Age range: 25–35 years old
- Context: Urban HK professional appearance — athletic casual wear, not gym kit, not office attire
- Social dynamic: Genuine interaction — a moment of laughter, conversation, or celebratory gesture between people who are comfortable with each other
- Group size: 2–3 people (intimate enough to feel like a real social moment, not a crowd)
- Demographic: Reflects Hong Kong's young professional community — East/Southeast Asian subjects or mixed group natural to HK context

**Flexible Attributes:**
- Exact expressions and poses (must remain in social-warm register)
- Precise clothing items and colors
- Gender composition of group
- Exact spatial positioning within scene

---

### entity_02 — Pickleball Paddles

**Preservation Priority:** High

**Recognizability Requirement:** Approximate Match

**Immutable Attributes:**
- Must be identifiable as pickleball paddles (not tennis rackets, not badminton rackets)
- Must be held casually — at side, in hand between rallies, not mid-swing aggressive posture
- Positioning should reinforce "between-play social moment," not athletic execution

**Flexible Attributes:**
- Brand/color of paddles (no specific paddle brand required unless client specifies)
- Exact number visible
- Angle and orientation

---

### entity_03 — Pickleball Court and Net

**Preservation Priority:** High

**Recognizability Requirement:** Approximate Match

**Immutable Attributes:**
- Court surface must be recognizable as a pickleball/multi-sport court (line markings)
- Net must be present and visible in scene
- Court should communicate an active, real venue — not an empty or abandoned space

**Flexible Attributes:**
- Court color (standard blue/green or venue-specific)
- How much of the court is visible
- Perspective angle of court lines

---

### entity_04 — Indoor Sports Venue Environment

**Preservation Priority:** Medium

**Recognizability Requirement:** Loose Match

**Immutable Attributes:**
- Must feel like an indoor sports facility — not a hotel ballroom, not a gym weight room
- Environment should have the energy of an active, community-oriented space
- Lighting must read as sports venue ambient lighting — not dramatic studio lighting

**Flexible Attributes:**
- Specific architectural details
- Ceiling height and structure
- Presence or absence of other players in background

---

### entity_05 — GigaSports Brand Element

**Preservation Priority:** High

**Recognizability Requirement:** Strong Match

**Immutable Attributes:**
- GigaSports logo or branded signage must be present and legible
- Must not dominate the frame or interrupt the primary social narrative
- Placement: Background environment (wall, banner, court perimeter) — not foreground overlay

**Flexible Attributes:**
- Size and exact placement within environment
- Whether it appears as signage, banner, or court marking

---

## Reality Model

**Type:** Realistic

**Coherence Rules:**
- Scale: Realistic — all entities proportional to real-world pickleball venue
- Perspective: Realistic — natural camera perspective, no forced distortion
- Anchoring: Required — all subjects floor-anchored or physically grounded within venue
- Environmental Behavior: Realistic — indoor sports venue lighting, natural spatial relationships

**Objective:** Reality-model coherence, not photographic perfection. The scene should read as a credible lifestyle photograph, not a hyper-real commercial product shot.

---

## Physical Construction

### Depth Structure

**Foreground:**
- entity_01 (subject group) — positioned in foreground-to-midground zone, large enough in frame that faces are readable
- entity_02 (paddles) — held by subjects in foreground, incidentally visible

**Midground:**
- entity_03 (court surface and net) — establishes sport context, anchors scene spatially

**Background:**
- entity_04 (venue environment) — provides environmental depth and mood
- entity_05 (GigaSports brand element) — present in background, legible but not dominant

**Required Entities:**
- entity_01: 2–3 young HK professionals in social interaction moment on court
- entity_02: Pickleball paddles (casually held)
- entity_03: Pickleball court surface with net

**Required Environment Elements:**
- entity_04: Indoor sports venue
- entity_05: GigaSports brand element in background

**Required Supporting Objects:**
- None beyond above

**Excluded Elements:**
- Pickleball in mid-air (implies active play, shifts focus to athletic execution)
- Solo subject (contradicts community/social concept)
- Studio backdrop or non-venue environment
- Overly formal or posed group positioning
