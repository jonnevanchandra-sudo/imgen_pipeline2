# Art Direction Contract
**Brand:** GigaSports
**Campaign:** Gigasports Pickle Club Community
**Framework:** Art Direction Framework v2.0
**Stage:** 4 — Visual Interpretation

---

## Inputs

**From Strategy Contract:**
- Strategic Direction: Active Networking — community-through-sport activation
- Identity Migration Vector: Screen-fatigued transactional networker → Active, casually confident community member

**From Narrative Contract:**
- NarrativeLensSelection: Human Connection (primary) + Modern Freedom (supporting)
- CurrentEmotionalState: Social fatigue and resignation — going through the motions
- PsychologicalFriction: Sport feels like a performance barrier; community feels inaccessible
- DesiredTransformation: Transactional obligation → Authentic belonging
- ViewerTakeaway: "These are people like me. This is the social life I actually want. I could belong here."

**Brand Preservation Guardrails (compliance only):**
- Sacred brand assets: Athlete-centric photography, community and event integration, commercial clarity
- Forbidden: Elite exclusivity, purely transactional retail framing, solo athletic heroism

---

## Visual Intent

**Primary:** Belonging

The dominant emotional-perceptual objective is belonging — the sense that this community already exists, that the people in it are recognizable peers, and that entering it is natural rather than earned.

**Supporting:** Human Warmth + Ease

The image should feel warm in a social sense — not romanticized, not studio-lit perfection, but the genuine warmth of a group of people who are comfortable with each other. And ease: everything in the visual should communicate that this is accessible, natural, and low-stakes.

---

## Visual Concept

**Social Encouragement in Motion**

What kind of image exists: A candid moment within a social game of pickleball — not the athletic execution of a shot, but the human moment that surrounds it. The laugh after a missed shot. The high-five. Two people mid-conversation with paddles in hand between rallies. The glance across the net that communicates "we're in this together."

The sport is the setting. The connection is the subject.

This is not a sports action photograph. This is a social photograph that happens to take place on a pickleball court.

**What this is NOT:**
- Not a hero athlete making a powerful shot
- Not a product-forward layout (paddles as focal point)
- Not a posed group photo with forced smiles
- Not gym-style intensity captured on a court

---

## Subject Relationship Logic

**Equality + Partnership**

The psychological relationship between viewer and scene should feel like looking at a group you could step into — not looking up at aspirational figures, not looking down at others. The subjects occupy the same social plane as the viewer.

This is not mentorship. This is not aspiration from a distance. This is recognition — "I could be here, with these people, right now."

The relationship between subjects within the scene should communicate **mutual enjoyment** — the kind of dynamic that forms between people who are sharing something physical and low-pressure together.

---

## Perceptual Meaning

> "This feels socially welcoming."

> "This feels like something I could actually join."

> "These people look like they're genuinely having fun — not performing fun."

> "This looks like my kind of people."

The viewer should feel the warmth of the scene before they read any copy. The image must do its emotional work independently of text.

---

## Attention Control

**Structural Density:** Medium

The scene should have enough environmental context to communicate "this is a real place with real people" — but not so much visual information that the human connection is buried in detail. The court, the environment, and supporting elements should remain legible but not compete for attention.

**Focal Priority:** Human faces and social interaction

The cognitive entry point must be the human connection between subjects — specifically the expression and body language of the group moment. This is the single element that cannot be lost or subordinated. The viewer's eye must land on human energy first, sport context second.

---

## Output Schema

**VisualIntent:** Belonging + Human Warmth + Ease

**VisualConcept:** Social Encouragement in Motion — a candid social moment on the pickleball court (between-play interaction, laughter, group connection) rather than athletic performance execution

**SubjectRelationshipLogic:** Equality and Partnership — viewer perceives themselves as a potential peer, not an aspirational observer; subjects within scene communicate mutual enjoyment and genuine social comfort

**PerceptualMeaning:** "This feels socially welcoming. These are people like me. I could belong here."

**AttentionControl:**
- Structural Density: Medium
- Focal Priority: Human faces and social interaction — must claim cognitive entry before sport context or environment
