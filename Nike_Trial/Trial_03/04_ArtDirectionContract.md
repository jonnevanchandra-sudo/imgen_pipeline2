# Art Direction Contract
**Brand:** Nike
**Campaign:** Nike Chill Run Club
**Framework:** Art Direction Framework v3
**Stage:** 4 — Visual Interpretation

---

## Inputs

**From Strategy Contract:**
- StrategicDirection: "The city at night is yours to claim — if you run through it with someone else who chose to show up."
- IdentityMigration: Drained Office Worker → Night City Protagonist (activation)
- ActivatedDimensions: Community Belonging (Critical), Individual Transformation (High), Accessibility (High)

**From Narrative Contract:**
- Primary Lens: Social Liberation — the run is shared; that's what makes it real
- Secondary Lens: Main Character Moment — HK night as cinematic backdrop for a version of yourself you don't give yourself permission to be
- DesiredTransformation: exhaustion → presence; isolation → social warmth; work identity → personal identity
- ViewerTakeaway: "That's what my city looks like at night when you go outside. We could do that."
- Narrative Requirement: Genuine social interaction must be visible — shared laugh, glance, comfortable social proximity of actual friends

**Brand Guardrails (from BrandContract):**
- Sacred assets: Athletic human subjects, editorial photography language, movement-focused imagery, premium realism
- Protected truth: Authenticity > Visual Spectacle; Community; Lifestyle-Integrated Sport; Emotional Storytelling > Product Explanation
- Forbidden: Elite athletic framing, exclusive or intimidating visual language, product-hero composition

## VisualIntent

**Shared Claim on the City**

Two people are running through Hong Kong at night and the city is theirs right now. The image communicates that this is genuinely the best part of their day — not because it's physically impressive or aesthetically produced, but because they're here together and they both chose this. The visual register is warm, real, and immediately recognizable. It should feel like something a friend photographed and posted, not an ad.

---

## VisualConcept

**夜跑主角 — Night Run Protagonists**

Two young HK professionals — mid-run through the city at night, city lights glowing behind them. The image reads as a genuine social moment that happens to involve running: they are in conversation, or mid-laugh, or sharing a glance that communicates something only the two of them know — the specific warmth of two people who made it out tonight and are glad they did.

The social energy is the visual headline. The HK night cityscape is their world at this hour. The Nike Alphafly 3 is on their feet as they move through it.

This is not "two people running near each other." This is two people running *with* each other — and the difference is visible.

---

## SubjectRelationshipLogic

**Active Social Connection**

The subjects are friends doing something they both chose. The relationship is visible in the image: a shared laugh mid-stride, or one glancing toward the other with a natural smile, or the easy physical proximity of people who are comfortable together. The social interaction is happening — not as performance for the camera, but as the actual state of two people genuinely enjoying an evening run.

The viewer's relationship to the scene is belonging and recognition — not aspiration toward an unattainable figure. The subjects must read as real HK young professionals: someone's age, someone's context, the same kind of week. The viewer thinks "that could be me and my friend" not "that's someone I'll never be."

The social interaction must not look staged or directed. It must look like what it is: two people running together at night in their city, and something funny or easy or warm just happened between them.

---

## AttentionControl

**Focal Priority:** The social dynamic between the two subjects must be the first thing the viewer processes — the warmth and ease of two people who are genuinely here together. The HK night reads second as the environment: this is their city at this hour. The Nike Alphafly 3 reads third as product recognition — present and legible, arrived at naturally.

**Structural Density:** Medium. Enough environmental context to clearly read HK at night — city lights, harbor glow, warm city atmosphere. But subjects dominate the frame. The city supports the social meaning; it does not compete with it.

The image should earn its second look — the first look is the social warmth; the second look is where you see the shoe and notice the Nike branding. That ordering is intentional.

---

## DistinctivenessValidation

**Evaluated against:**
- Generic fitness stock photography: group running in formation, faces forward, performance posture
- Instagram influencer running content: solo runner, aspirational body, motivational overlay
- Performance running ads: speed, motion blur, achievement framing
- Nike's own performance-oriented running campaigns: athletic intensity, competitive context

**Differentiation:**
This image must not read as a running advertisement. It must read as a social moment that was photographed — a specific warm exchange between two people who are mid-run through Hong Kong at night. The movement is the vehicle. The friendship is the story. The city is the backdrop that makes it feel like theirs.

**Anti-patterns confirmed avoided:**
- No parallel runners facing forward with no interaction between them
- No athletic intensity or competitive posture
- No aspirational fitness framing or body emphasis
- No heroic staging or motivational pose
- No studio or artificial lighting that reads as produced
- No solo run narrative — the social dimension is non-negotiable per the brief
