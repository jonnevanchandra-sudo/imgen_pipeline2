# Narrative Contract
**Brand:** Nike
**Campaign:** Nike Chill Run Club — Trial 04
**Framework:** Narrative Framework
**Stage:** 3 — Emotional Interpretation

---

## Emotional Arc

**CurrentEmotionalState**
The viewer is at the end of a long day. Work drained them. Their body is tired but their mind is still running — replaying the day, the messages, the obligations. They are in that specific HK working-life state of being simultaneously exhausted and unable to switch off. They haven't moved for themselves all day. The city outside feels like it belongs to other people.

**PsychologicalFriction**
The internal resistance: *I'm too tired. I'm not a runner. I wouldn't know where to start. I'd look out of place.* The friction is not physical — it is identity-based. Running feels like something that belongs to a different kind of person: one with more time, more discipline, more athletic history than they have.

**DesiredTransformation**
The image does not argue with the friction. It bypasses it. The viewer sees two people in motion in HK at night — relaxed, connected, clearly not racing — and something shifts. The run they are watching is not an athletic event. It is just two people who decided that tonight, the city is theirs. The Alphafly 3 on their feet is not intimidating; it is aspirational in the specific way of: *that is what caring about your run looks like.*

**IdentityArrival**
The viewer sees themselves in subject_01. They see someone they admire — Karina — beside that person. The image quietly says: if you showed up tonight, you would belong here. The run is not a test. It is a reset. The door is open.

**ViewerTakeaway**
*I could do this. Tonight. In the right shoes. With someone worth running with.*

---

## Narrative Lens

**Primary Lens: The Shared Reset**
The emotional center is the mid-run moment between two people — not the start, not the finish, but the somewhere-in-the-middle where the day falls away and you are simply running with someone beside you. This is the most honest version of what the Chill Run Club offers: the company, the city, the movement, the exhale.

**Secondary Lens: The Chosen Run**
The Alphafly 3 is a choice that the subjects made. Wearing a racing-pedigree shoe to a social chill run is a statement of care: *I showed up properly.* Not to compete — to commit. This lens makes the shoe meaningful without making the image about the shoe.

---

## Emotional Intensity

**Level: Medium**

The image should feel warm, alive, and present — not euphoric, not adrenaline-charged. The energy is the specific quality of being mid-run with good company in a city you love at night. Elevated, not explosive. The viewer should feel invited, not intimidated.

---

## Narrative Constraints

- Must NOT carry competitive or race energy — no sense of pace pressure, no finish-line urgency
- Must NOT reduce either subject to a brand spokesperson or product model — both are runners first
- Must NOT sentimentalize — the emotion is clean and immediate, not nostalgic or dramatic
- The Alphafly 3 is present as a natural part of the run, not as a featured product moment
