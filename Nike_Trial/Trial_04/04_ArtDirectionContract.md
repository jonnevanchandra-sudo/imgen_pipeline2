# Art Direction Contract
**Brand:** Nike
**Campaign:** Nike Chill Run Club — Trial 04
**Framework:** Art Direction Framework v3
**Stage:** 4 — Visual Interpretation

---

## Visual Intent

The image should feel like it was taken by someone running alongside them — not from the outside looking in, but from inside the run. Two people in motion in HK at night. The city is present but theirs. The Alphafly 3 is visible on their feet — recognized, not announced.

The emotional register is: *this is what it looks like when you decide the evening belongs to you.*

---

## Visual Concept

**"夜跑搭檔" — Running Partners at Night**

Two subjects — subject_01 (user) and subject_02 (Karina from aespa) — caught mid-run in a Hong Kong urban environment at evening or night. Neither is racing the other. They are running together — that specific quality of shared pace and shared presence where the city backdrop and the movement become one thing.

The visual concept is not a pose. It is a moment: both subjects in genuine motion, at a conversational pace, side by side or in easy near-contact proximity. The Alphafly 3 is visible on their feet as a natural part of the run — not isolated, not highlighted, but clearly present and identifiable to a viewer who recognizes the shoe.

---

## Subject Relationship Logic

**Type: Running Companions — Equal and Present**

Neither subject is positioned as the primary or secondary figure in terms of narrative importance — both are running, both carry the same emotional quality. The image does not separate them into "star" and "supporting role." They are two people who chose to run together tonight.

The relational dynamic between them is low-key warmth: they are at a pace where they could talk, they are aware of each other, they are not performing for the camera. The run is the subject; they are in it.

---

## Perceptual Meaning

What the viewer should feel looking at this image before reading a single word:

- *Two people running in Hong Kong at night.*
- *They look like they're enjoying it.*
- *Those shoes are serious.*
- *I want to be out there.*

The image bypasses the question "should I run?" and replaces it with the feeling of already being in the run.

---

## Attention Control

**Primary:** Subject faces and body language — motion, warmth, presence
**Secondary:** The Alphafly 3 on their feet — shoe is legible, not spotlit
**Supporting:** HK night environment — city lights, urban texture, the city at their backs

The face carries the emotion. The shoe carries the product identity. The city carries the cultural permission — this is HK, this is your city, this run is available to you.

---

## Anti-Patterns

- No posed stop for the camera — both subjects are in genuine motion
- No athletic grimace or maximum-effort expression — the pace is social, the faces carry ease
- No product isolation or shoe close-up that removes the shoe from the body in motion
- No generic night city — must be recognizably Hong Kong
- No celebrity-shoot staging that places Karina above the human register — she is a runner here, not a brand face
- No matching outfits or coordinated looks — varied, individual, natural
