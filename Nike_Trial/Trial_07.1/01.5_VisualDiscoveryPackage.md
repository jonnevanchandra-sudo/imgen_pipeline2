## Boundary Check

- ✓ 6 archetypes generated, each genuinely distinct (warm night / documentary / cinematic / neon / green calm / golden hour).
- ✓ Every `google_image_query` includes explicit photography-style/genre terms, not just mood words.
- ✓ No archetype conflicts with `BrandContract.SacredBrandAssets` — all keep human-first hierarchy, restraint, and authentic realism; none make the shoe the hero or use studio polish.
- ✓ `client_prompt` is plain-language and actionable for a non-creative client.
- ✓ Does NOT define VisualIntent/VisualConcept or make selections — those belong to Stage 4 / Stage 5.5. Pipeline proceeds to Stage 2 immediately.
