# Art Direction Contract
**Brand:** Nike
**Campaign:** Nike Chill Run Club
**Framework:** Art Direction Framework v2.0
**Stage:** 4 — Visual Interpretation

---

## Inputs

**From Strategy Contract:**
- Strategic Direction: "Your body already knows how to leave the day behind. The Novablast 5 is what liberation feels like underfoot."
- Key activated dimension: Individual Agency (Critical) + Kinesthetic Freedom (High)
- Identity Migration Vector: Digital Hostage → Person in Motion

**From Narrative Contract:**
- NarrativeLensSelection: Kinesthetic Liberation (primary) + Present State Revelation (secondary)
- CurrentEmotionalState: Cognitively overloaded, physically inert, day unresolved in the body
- DesiredTransformation: Stillness → motion → the discovery that the body was not tired, only waiting
- ViewerTakeaway: That lightness is available to me. The Novablast 5 is how it starts.
- Emotional Intensity: Medium — quiet liberation, not euphoria

**From Brand Contract (as guardrail):**
- Sacred Brand Assets: Editorial photography language, movement as identity signal, premium realism, strong negative space
- Creative Tradeoff: Authenticity > Visual Spectacle; Emotional Storytelling > Product Explanation
- Forbidden: Aspirational fitness body, elite exclusivity, product-hero composition

## VisualIntent

**Liberation through stride.** The image should feel like the specific second when a body stops carrying the workday and starts carrying itself. Not the social warmth of running in a group, not the satisfaction of finishing a run — the pure kinesthetic moment of motion finding its rhythm. Two people, side by side, neither performing for the other — each fully inside their own stride. The emotional-perceptual objective: the viewer watches two people whose bodies have already solved the problem the viewer hasn't started yet.

---

## VisualConcept

**"In Stride"** — two runners at Hong Kong harbor at golden hour, caught mid-stride in synchronized motion. The frame catches them in the specific second of full extension — bodies long, one foot forward, one behind, the weight of the day visibly absent from their faces. This is not a running photograph. It is a photograph of two people who have remembered what their bodies can do.

The Nike Novablast 5 is visible on both subjects' feet — mid-stride, the distinctive ZOOMX midsole profile catches the warm afternoon light. The shoe is not posed or displayed. It is doing what it was made to do, and that doing is the visual argument.

The subjects are **Karina (from aespa)** and **the user (reference image provided)**. They are not interacting with each other. They are running parallel, at the same pace, in the same direction. The connection between them is rhythm, not conversation.

---

## SubjectRelationshipLogic

**Parallel Liberation.** The psychological dynamic between subjects is not mutual connection (Trial 1's social warmth between peers) and not the newcomer/welcome dynamic (Giga Trial 4) — it is something quieter: two people who are each fully inside their own experience of freedom, and who happen to be sharing it spatially. They are not running together in the social sense. They are running in the same direction at the same hour, and that coincidence of motion is its own form of companionship.

The emotional transaction between them is not communicated through eye contact, gesture, or social body language. It is communicated through synchronized stride. The viewer perceives the connection through the rhythm — their bodies move in harmony without negotiating it.

---

## PerceptualMeaning

**"These two people have completely left their day behind. I can see it in the way they're moving."**

The image communicates two things simultaneously: (1) motion is the reset — the body is the mechanism, not willpower or social energy; (2) the Novablast 5 is what that reset feels like underfoot — visible, mid-stride, part of the liberation. The viewer should feel: if I were wearing those shoes, running at that hour, in that city, I would also look like that. And I want to.

---

## AttentionControl

**Structural Density:** Low–Medium. Clean composition, strong negative space in the HK golden hour sky behind the subjects. The subjects and their shoes dominate — the environment is warm light and atmosphere, not architectural detail.

**Focal Priority Sequence:**
1. Subjects' faces — the expression of present-state liberation (no tension, no task-mode)
2. The Novablast 5 mid-stride — the ZOOMX midsole profile and Volt Swoosh catching the light
3. The synchronized stride of both subjects — the rhythm that communicates parallel liberation
4. Hong Kong harbor golden hour — the city as the backdrop of reclaimed time

**Shoe visibility requirement:** The Novablast 5 must be clearly visible mid-stride on at least one subject's foot — the distinctive rounded ZOOMX midsole profile is the shoe's visual identity. The camera angle must allow this profile to read. This is an art direction constraint driven by the ProductSpec: the shoe is part of the motion narrative and must be legible as the specific shoe it is.

---

## DistinctivenessValidation

**Differentiation from Trial 1:** Trial 1 showed a group of 3–4 subjects running toward the camera in social formation at blue hour / night. The subjects were engaged with each other — laughing, glancing sideways, performing social ease. The emotional argument was collective: running is fun with your people. Trial 2 shows 2 subjects running parallel at golden hour, not engaged with each other — each inside their own liberation. The emotional argument is individual: your body already knows how to do this. The shoe, the hour, and the motion do the work.

**Differentiation from category tropes:**
- Not a group shot radiating social warmth (already done in Trial 1)
- Not a solo hero shot with a dramatic Nike sky (aspirational performance, contradicts brief)
- Not a product flat-lay or shoe close-up (brand forbids product-hero composition)
- Not a finish-line or achievement moment (contradicts kinesthetic liberation framing)

The specific novelty: two subjects in synchronized motion, the shoe visible mid-stride at golden hour, faces in the expression of pure present-moment awareness. Underused in lifestyle running advertising.
