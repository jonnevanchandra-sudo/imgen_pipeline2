# Art Direction Contract
**Brand:** Nike
**Campaign:** Nike Chill Run Club
**Framework:** Art Direction Framework v2.0
**Stage:** 4 — Visual Interpretation

---

## Inputs

**From Strategy Contract:**
- StrategicDirection: "Running as Social Reset"
- IdentityMigrationVector: activation — Drained Office Worker → Active Social Person

**From Narrative Contract:**
- NarrativeLensSelection: Human Connection (primary) + Emotional Recovery (secondary)
- CurrentEmotionalState: Post-work identity depletion; belief that social activity is one more demand
- PsychologicalFriction: "I am too tired, and I am not the kind of person who runs with other people"
- DesiredTransformation: exhaustion → vitality; isolation → genuine belonging; work identity → personal identity
- IdentityArrival: Active Social Person — someone with a life beyond the office, present in their own evenings
- ViewerTakeaway: "These are people exactly like me — genuinely having fun — and I could belong here"

**Brand Guardrails (from BrandContract):**
- Sacred assets: Athletic human subjects, editorial photography language, movement-focused imagery, premium realism
- Protected truth: Authenticity > Visual Spectacle; Lifestyle-Integrated Sport; Emotional Storytelling > Product Explanation
- Forbidden: Elite athletic framing, exclusive or intimidating visual language

---

## VisualIntent

**Effortless Social Vitality**

The image communicates that this group run is the most natural, easy, enjoyable thing these people could be doing right now. Not effort. Not sport. Not performance. Life — their actual social life, reclaimed from an evening that could have ended in isolation.

The visual register is warm, real, and immediately recognizable to the target audience. The image should not feel like advertising. It should feel like something a friend photographed and posted.

---

## VisualConcept

**The Reclaimed Evening**

A group of young HK professionals in motion together — running through a Hong Kong evening environment. The image reads as a social moment that happens to involve running — not a running moment that involves social interaction.

The subjects are visibly alive and connected to each other. Their energy is warm and easy — not athletic focus, not effort concentration. They are mid-conversation, mid-laugh, or simply running in the comfortable proximity of people they genuinely like being around. The movement is the vehicle. The social warmth is the message.

The Hong Kong environment behind them — waterfront, urban park, or city path at evening — signals that this is happening in their city, their evening, their time. HK is the home of this experience, not a backdrop.

---

## SubjectRelationshipLogic

**Belonging + Equality**

The viewer's psychological relationship to the scene is belonging — not observation, not aspiration toward an unreachable figure. The subjects are not performing for the camera. They are absorbed in their group. The viewer sees themselves as someone who could step into this group — someone their same age, their same context, with the same kind of job and the same kind of week.

The subjects must not read as athletes or fitness identities. They must read as people — HK young professionals who have found something good to do with their Tuesday evening.

The camera relationship communicates equality: viewer and subjects occupy the same plane. Neither observing from above nor looking up at aspirational figures. Standing alongside.

---

## PerceptualMeaning

- "This is something real people like me actually do after work in Hong Kong."
- "This group looks genuinely happy — not posed, not polished, not performing for a brand."
- "This looks like a social life I actually want — not an idealized one I could never access."
- "This looks easy enough that I could join without being the wrong kind of person."
- "These are my people."

---

## AttentionControl

**Structural Density:** Medium
Enough environmental context to read Hong Kong evening — the city is a character in the image, not decoration. But subjects dominate the frame. The environment supports the social meaning; it does not compete with it.

**Focal Priority:** The social dynamic between the running subjects must claim immediate cognitive priority.

The first thing the viewer processes must be human faces and the energy between them — who is laughing, how they are positioned relative to each other, the warmth of their proximity. The Hong Kong environment is read second, as the answer to "where is this happening." Nike branding is perceived third, as confirmation of the brand — not as the reason for looking.

---

## DistinctivenessValidation

**Evaluated against:**
- Generic fitness stock photography (group running in a row, faces forward, performance stances)
- Instagram influencer running content (solo runner, filter-heavy, aspirational body)
- Performance running ads (speed, motion blur, achievement framing)
- Nike's own performance-oriented running campaigns

**Differentiation:**
This image must not feel like a running advertisement. It must feel like a moment from someone's actual social life that happened to be photographed. The movement is secondary to the social energy. The subjects are peers, not aspirational figures. The environment is recognizably Hong Kong — not generic urban or studio.

**Anti-patterns confirmed avoided:**
- No heroic solo running shot
- No finish-line or achievement imagery
- No aspirational fitness body language or formation
- No motivational staging or posed group lineup
- No performance focus or athletic intensity
- No generic park or treadmill setting
