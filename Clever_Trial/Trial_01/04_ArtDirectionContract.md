# Art Direction Contract
**Brand:** CLEVER Protein
**Campaign:** The 3 PM Office Rescue (3點鐘辦公室救星)
**Framework:** Art Direction Framework v2.0
**Stage:** 4 — Visual Interpretation

---

## Inputs

**From Strategy Contract:**
- StrategicDirection: "The Smart 3pm Swap"
- IdentityMigrationVector: activation — Office worker caught in the 3pm willpower battle → Office worker who has quietly 'solved' the 3pm problem

**From Narrative Contract:**
- NarrativeLensSelection: Calm Mastery (primary) + Quiet Confidence (secondary)
- CurrentEmotionalState: The 3pm lull — restless, mildly hungry, intentions feel negotiable, the snack-run ritual feels automatic
- PsychologicalFriction: Choosing differently has historically felt like opting out — less satisfaction, more effort, social separateness
- DesiredTransformation: restless/tempted → settled/decided; anticipated friction → quiet ease; "I have to resist" → "I already chose"
- IdentityArrival: Someone for whom the 3pm moment has quietly stopped being a daily test — understated competence, nothing visibly "happened"
- ViewerTakeaway: "Oh — that's just the easy swap. I could just do that."

**Brand Guardrails (from BrandContract):**
- Sacred assets: CLEVER wordmark, soft pink/rose + white palette, Weight Down packaging, translucency/clarity as purity motif
- Protected truth: Calm, restrained, quietly confident tone; "Clever choice" as the central decision narrative; CLEVER side of any comparison carries the brand's clean visual language
- Forbidden: Clinical/sterile supplement aesthetic, hardcore athletic framing, guilt/shame-based weight-loss imagery

---

## VisualIntent

**Calm Decisiveness**

The image communicates that a decision has quietly already been made — without effort, without announcement, without anyone watching. The emotional register is settled and unhurried, not triumphant or instructive. The viewer should feel like they're looking at an ordinary mid-afternoon moment that happens to contain a small, smart choice — not a staged demonstration of willpower or a before/after transformation.

---

## VisualConcept

**The Quiet Swap**

A single mid-afternoon office moment in which two options for the 3pm break exist within the same world — the familiar snack-run option (siu mai, fish balls, iced lemon tea — the kind of order that shows up at a desk without much thought) and the CLEVER Weight Down product, present and already part of the scene, as the thing that's actually been chosen.

The concept is not a competitive showdown between two equals fighting for attention — it's a moment of quiet resolution where one option has already receded into "the thing I'm not doing today" and the other has simply, calmly, become "what's happening." The contrast exists, but it reads as settled rather than dramatized.

This concept is compatible with a side-by-side or near/far visual relationship between the two options, but Art Direction does not prescribe the spatial layout, framing, or graphic treatment of that relationship — that belongs to Scene Assembly and Composition.

---

## SubjectRelationshipLogic

**Recognition**

The viewer's relationship to the scene is recognition, not observation of a stranger and not aspiration toward an idealized figure. This is meant to read as "my desk, my 3pm, my version of this moment" — whether or not a person is visibly present, the scene should feel inhabited and ordinary, like a moment captured mid-afternoon rather than staged for a camera.

If a human presence is included (e.g. a hand, a partial figure), the relationship dynamic is equality and ordinariness — someone going about their afternoon, not someone performing a lifestyle for an audience.

---

## PerceptualMeaning

- "This is the moment I'm in right now."
- "The smart option is just... already there, already part of this moment — not a special intervention."
- "This doesn't feel like a sacrifice — it feels like noticing something that was already obvious."
- "This feels calm and unhurried, not preachy or instructional."
- "The CLEVER product looks clean, considered, and quietly confident next to the other option."

---

## AttentionControl

**Structural Density:** Low-to-Medium
The scene should feel like an ordinary desk/office moment — enough detail to read "afternoon, workplace, real life" — but not cluttered or busy. Visual calm supports the "quiet mastery" register; a noisy, maximalist scene would contradict it.

**Focal Priority:** The CLEVER Weight Down product (packaging and/or prepared shake) must claim immediate cognitive priority as the "resolved" choice — the thing the eye settles on. The comparison snack/drink should be present and recognizable but should read as secondary, peripheral, or already-passed-over, supporting the narrative's "already decided" emotional state rather than competing equally for attention.

---

## DistinctivenessValidation

**Evaluated against:**
- Generic before/after weight-loss imagery (scales, tape measures, body-comparison framing)
- Clinical supplement-lab product photography (sterile white backgrounds, lab equipment, pill-bottle aesthetics)
- Guilt-driven "junk food shaming" close-ups common in diet-culture social content
- Generic influencer flat-lay aesthetics overused in HK fitness Instagram content

**Differentiation:**
This image should feel like a candid slice of an ordinary HK workday — specific enough (3pm, desk, familiar snack-run items) to be instantly recognizable to the target audience, but rendered with CLEVER's calm, uncluttered visual register rather than a loud comparison-graphic or diet-culture treatment. The "smart choice" message should land through quiet visual emphasis, not through labels, scoreboards, or dramatized contrast.

**Anti-patterns confirmed avoided:**
- No body-transformation or weight-scale imagery
- No clinical/lab supplement aesthetic
- No shame-framed close-ups of the comparison food
- No loud graphic "VS" scoreboard treatment
- No staged, performative lifestyle posing

---

## CompatibilityValidation

- CompatibleWithBrandIdentity: True — activates Confidence (Clever-choice framing), Accessibility (everyday office context), and Performance (product visibly present as the credible choice) without contradicting the brand's calm/restrained tone
- CompatibleWithNarrativeMeaning: True — "The Quiet Swap" directly embodies the desired transformation (restless/tempted → settled/decided) and the "already chosen" identity arrival
- PreservesDownstreamFlexibility: True — no camera mechanics, lighting behavior, composition structure, spatial layout, or rendering behavior specified; the near/far or side-by-side relationship is named conceptually but left to Scene Assembly and Composition to construct
- NoRenderingContamination: True — no lighting direction, depth of field, color temperature, texture, or atmospheric behavior prescribed
