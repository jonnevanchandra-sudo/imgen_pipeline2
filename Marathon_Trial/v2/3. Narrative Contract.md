Narrative Contract
Campaign Narrative Theme: Guided Fit Confidence
Brand: Marathon Sports HK / 馬拉松
Narrative Version: 1.0
Layer: Narrative / Emotional Interpretation
Primary Emotional Job: Turn running-shoe anxiety into calm, supported confidence.


1. Inputs

Brand Contract Inputs

Marathon Sports HK should be emotionally understood as a trusted local sports retail gateway: energetic, local, practical, inclusive, community-minded, and helpful rather than distant or premium-coded.

The brand’s confidence style comes through product benefits, community proof, staff warmth, and easy entry, not superiority or luxury distance.

The brand also has a strong multi-brand curation role. Partner brands such as Nike, ASICS, Hoka, adidas, and others provide product breadth, but Marathon Sports HK must remain the local curator that makes that breadth useful and relevant.

Strategy Contract Inputs

The strategy is to drive free fitting bookings and qualified store visits from casual, beginner, and returning recreational runners in Hong Kong.

The strategic shift is from brand guessing and purchase anxiety into a guided, low-pressure fitting behavior that feels useful, easy, and confidence-building.

The key behavioral frame is:

Running shoe buying is a guided fit decision, not a guessing game or a single-brand gamble.

Marathon Sports HK’s role is the trusted local multi-brand guide that helps runners compare options under one roof without brand pushing or pressure.

Required Strategic Meaning

The narrative must preserve:

guided fit confidence
stop guessing
find your fit
free fitting
expert fitting
multi-brand comparison
no pressure
beginner-friendly guidance
comfort confidence

The narrative must avoid:

one-brand-fits-all thinking
pressure sales
elite-only specialist language
technical gatekeeping
guaranteed injury prevention
medicalized claims
specific shoe-model recommendations without assessment


2. Narrative Lens Selection

Primary Lens: Quiet Confidence

Question:
How can confidence be expressed without dominance?

Narrative Role:
The runner does not become a superior athlete or expert. They become quietly reassured: someone who can make a better decision because they are supported.

This is the strongest fit because the strategy is not asking for an aggressive performance story. It is asking for reassurance, clarity, and guided decision-making.

Secondary Lens: Effortless Capability

Question:
How can achievement feel attainable?

Narrative Role:
The fitting experience makes the next step feel easy. The audience does not need to decode every shoe technology or commit to one brand before entering the store.

Support Lens: Human Connection

Question:
Which relationship creates meaning?

Narrative Role:
Staff expertise becomes emotionally important because the runner is not alone in the decision. The relationship is not “expert above beginner,” but “local guide beside runner.”

Emotional Intensity: Medium

The emotional volume should be clear but controlled. The story should have recognizable emotional movement without becoming overly cinematic, mythic, fear-based, or dramatic.


3. Current Emotional State

The viewer begins as an overwhelmed shoe guesser.

They are likely a casual, beginner, or returning Hong Kong runner who wants to run more comfortably but feels stuck before purchase.

They see too many brands, models, opinions, shoe technologies, and online recommendations. Nike, ASICS, Hoka, and other brands create excitement, but also make the decision feel heavier because each brand seems to ask for commitment.

Emotionally, they are not hopeless. They are interested but hesitant.

They may already want to run, restart running, or improve comfort, but the shoe decision feels expensive, technical, and easy to get wrong.

The emotional condition is:

unsure
overloaded
comparison-fatigued
purchase-risk aware
slightly intimidated
open to help, but resistant to pressure

The viewer is not lacking motivation. They are lacking confidence in the decision.


4. Psychological Friction

The core psychological friction is decision burden.

The runner is not blocked by lack of desire. They are blocked by the feeling that the decision has too many consequences: wasting money, choosing the wrong brand, getting discomfort, looking inexperienced, or being sold something they do not understand.

Primary Friction

“I don’t know enough to choose well.”

Secondary Frictions

Choice overload:
Too many brands and models make comparison feel impossible.

Financial risk:
The cost of running shoes makes the wrong choice feel like an expensive mistake.

Beginner intimidation:
The runner worries the store experience may be too technical, judgmental, or designed only for serious athletes.

Hype pressure:
Global brand excitement can become anxiety when the runner feels expected to choose based on loyalty, trend, or online opinion.

Comfort concern:
The runner associates poor fit with blisters, soreness, shin splints, knee discomfort, or reduced confidence, but the narrative must frame this as fit-supported comfort rather than medical prevention or cure.

Action delay:
Because the choice feels complicated, the runner postpones the decision instead of visiting or booking.

The required tension is:

guessing anxiety → guided confidence

Supported by:

choice overload → clear comparison
purchase risk → de-risked decision
beginner intimidation → accessible guidance
global brand breadth → local fit relevance
discomfort concern → fit-supported comfort
social-feed delay → simple booking action


5. Desired Transformation

Core Transformation

From:

“I might choose wrong.”

To:

“I can find what fits me.”

This is not a transformation into elite performance. It is a transformation into calm decision confidence.

The emotional movement should feel like:

overload → clarity
hesitation → reassurance
brand pressure → personal fit
purchase anxiety → supported choice
beginner intimidation → accessible guidance
guessing → guided comparison

The viewer should feel that expertise is available without judgment, comparison is possible without pressure, and running can begin or continue from a more confident place.

Emotional Resolution

The resolution is not:

“Buy this model.”
“Become a serious runner.”
“This shoe prevents injury.”
“This brand is best.”

The resolution is:

“You do not need to solve the shoe decision alone. Marathon Sports HK can help you compare, understand, and choose based on your own feet, stride, goals, and Hong Kong running context.”

That keeps the transformation psychologically grounded and aligned with the campaign’s free fitting behavior.


6. Identity Arrival

The viewer arrives as a confident fit-led runner.

They are not necessarily faster, more advanced, or more technical. They are someone who feels more capable because the decision has become understandable.

Arrival Identity

A supported, self-aware runner who chooses by fit, not guesswork.

Emotional Qualities

quietly confident
guided, not pressured
informed, not overloaded
beginner-safe
locally supported
practical and ready
more willing to visit, book, try, and compare

Identity Meaning

The viewer becomes someone who can say:

“I don’t have to know every shoe technology before I start. I can get help, try different brands, and choose what fits me.”

This fits Marathon Sports HK’s emotional role as the gear-savvy local guide who makes sport feel easier, more communal, and more accessible.


7. Viewer Takeaway

Primary Viewer Takeaway

“I don’t need to guess. I can find my fit at Marathon Sports HK.”

Emotional Residue

The viewer should leave with a feeling of relief and readiness.

The campaign should reduce mental load, not intensify fear.

The final emotional impression should be:

“This feels easy enough to try.”

Behavioral Takeaway

The emotional takeaway should naturally support the strategic action:

Visit Marathon Sports HK this weekend or book a free fitting now.

9. Narrative Boundaries

This contract must not define:

visual concepts
visual metaphors
subject placement
camera
lighting
color treatment
rendering style
production execution

The Narrative layer owns emotional meaning, psychological friction, identity experience, and viewer takeaway only.

Execution belongs to downstream layers.

Must Preserve

Marathon Sports HK / 馬拉松 as campaign owner and trusted guide.
Free fitting as the visible behavioral bridge.
Multi-brand comparison as reassurance, not confusion.
Staff expertise as approachable help.
Beginner-friendly, non-jargon emotional accessibility.
Hong Kong local relevance.
Comfort confidence without medical or injury-prevention guarantees.
Confidence without superiority.

Must Avoid

“One shoe for every runner.”
“This brand is best.”
“Buy this model now” without fitting context.
Elite-only performance language.
Medicalized pain or injury claims.
Fear escalation around injury.
Pressure-sales framing.
Generic global sports inspiration with no Hong Kong relevance.
Overwritten hero transformation.


10. Compressed Narrative Thesis

Marathon Sports HK helps unsure Hong Kong runners stop guessing and start choosing with confidence — not by pushing one brand, but by making fit feel clear, personal, local, and easy to act on.
