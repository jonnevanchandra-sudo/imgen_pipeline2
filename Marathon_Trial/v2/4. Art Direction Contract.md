          poor fit may affect comfort or confidence.
        ActivationTrigger: >
          The realization that guessing is unnecessary because Marathon Sports HK offers
          free expert fitting with one-store, multi-brand comparison.

    NarrativeContract:

      NarrativeLensSelection:
        PrimaryLens: Quiet Confidence
        SecondaryLens: Effortless Capability
        SupportLens: Human Connection
        EmotionalIntensity: Medium
        HumanMeaningDomains:
          - Confidence
          - Clarity
          - Belonging
          - Calm competence
          - Accessible self-improvement

      CurrentEmotionalState: >
        The viewer starts overwhelmed by running shoe choice, unsure which brand to trust,
        cautious about wasting money, and hesitant to enter a fitting environment that may
        feel too technical or serious. They are interested in running, but the buying decision
        feels heavier than the running itself.

      PsychologicalFriction: >
        The viewer believes they lack enough knowledge to choose correctly. Their friction
        comes from choice overload, purchase-risk anxiety, beginner intimidation, brand hype,
        and concern that poor fit may affect comfort or confidence. The friction is not
        laziness; it is decision uncertainty.

      DesiredTransformation: >
        The viewer moves from guessing to guided comparison. The emotional shift is from
        “I might choose wrong” to “I can be helped toward what fits me.” The transformation
        should feel reassuring, practical, locally accessible, and low-pressure.

      ViewerTakeaway: >
        I do not need to guess or commit to one brand first. Marathon Sports HK can help me
        find the shoe that fits me.

    BrandPreservationGuardrails:

      MustPreserve:
        - Marathon Sports HK / 馬拉松 as campaign owner, curator, and trusted guide.
        - Free fitting as the visible behavioral bridge.
        - Multi-brand comparison as reassurance, not confusion.
        - Staff expertise as approachable help.
        - Beginner-friendly, non-jargon emotional accessibility.
        - Hong Kong local relevance.
        - Comfort confidence without medical or injury-prevention guarantees.
        - Confidence without superiority.

      MustAvoid:
        - One-brand-fits-all thinking.
        - Partner brand dominance.
        - Pressure-sales framing.
        - Elite-only specialist language.
        - Technical gatekeeping.
        - Guaranteed injury prevention.
        - Medicalized claims.
        - Specific shoe-model recommendations without assessment.
        - Generic global sports inspiration with no Hong Kong relevance.

  VisualConcept:

    CoreConcept: Guided Choice Made Clear

    ConceptType:
      - Guided Experience
      - Human Connection
      - Everyday Transformation
      - Mentor Relationship
      - Lifestyle Integration

    ConceptDefinition: >
      A visual concept where the uncertainty of too many running-shoe options is transformed
      into a clear, personal, no-pressure decision through Marathon Sports HK’s local fitting
      guidance. The core image idea is not “the best shoe wins”; it is “the right guidance
      makes the choice feel manageable.”

    PsychologicalImageKind: >
      A human-centered fit-discovery experience where comparison, guidance, and personal
      comfort confidence are the emotional focus. The viewer should understand that the
      runner is not being pushed toward a single brand or model, but being supported toward
      a fit-led decision.

    SymbolicVisualFraming:
      - Multi-brand choice represents possibility, not confusion.
      - Staff expertise represents reassurance, not authority distance.
      - The fitting behavior represents the bridge from guessing to confidence.
      - The runner’s own fit represents the final organizing principle.
      - Marathon Sports HK represents the local curator that makes variety useful.
      - Free fitting represents the simple next step from hesitation to action.

    StrategicInterpretation: >
      The visual concept should make “Stop guessing. Find your fit.” feel immediate,
      practical, and emotionally safe. The image should express that Marathon Sports HK
      turns a high-consideration shoe decision into an accessible guided experience.

    MustNotBecome:
      - A single-brand endorsement.
      - A hero-shoe product worship image.
      - A generic runner-in-city lifestyle scene.
      - A technical clinic that feels intimidating.
      - A medical or injury-prevention promise.
      - A race-day performance fantasy.
      - A luxury retail image built on distance or exclusivity.
      - A sales-pressure moment disguised as expertise.


  SubjectRelationshipLogic:

    PrimaryRelationship: Local guide beside runner

    RelationshipMode:
      - Guidance
      - Support
      - Equality
      - Partnership
      - Emotional trust
      - Beginner-safe encouragement

    ViewerRelationshipToScene: >
      The viewer should feel, “Someone like me can get help here.” The scene should not make
      the viewer feel behind, inexperienced, or judged. It should make them feel invited into
      the decision.

    StaffRunnerDynamic:
      ExpertiseRole: >
        Staff expertise should feel approachable and collaborative. The staff member is not
        positioned psychologically above the runner, but beside them as a guide who makes the
        choice easier to understand.
      RunnerRole: >
        The runner should feel curious, supported, and increasingly reassured. Their uncertainty
        should be treated as normal, not as a weakness.
      DecisionOwnership: >
        The final choice belongs to the runner. Guidance supports confidence but does not remove
        personal agency.
      PressureLevel: No pressure

    ProductRelationship:
      ShoeRole: >
        Shoes are options to understand and compare, not answers being forced onto the runner.
      BrandRole: >
        Nike, ASICS, Hoka, adidas, and other partner brands may exist as examples of breadth,
        but they must not dominate Marathon Sports HK’s curator role.
      ComparisonMeaning: >
        Multi-brand comparison should feel like clarity under one roof, not a competition
        between brands or a wall of confusing choices.

    BrandRelationship:
      MarathonSportsHKRole: >
        Marathon Sports HK / 馬拉松 is the trusted local guide that turns product breadth into
        fit-led confidence.
      EmotionalAuthorityStyle: >
        Helpful, local, practical, inclusive, energetic, and warm rather than premium-coded,
        distant, technical-only, or elite.
