ArtDirectionContract:
  CampaignName: "Marathon Sports HK Free Running-Shoe Fitting"
  ThemeToken: "GuidedFitConfidence"
  LayerOwnership: "Visual Interpretation"
  FrameworkBoundary: >
    This contract defines the psychological visual concept and perceptual
    organization of the campaign. It does not define scene layout, camera,
    lighting, composition, rendering style, production workflow, or final asset execution.

  Inputs:
    StrategyInputs:
      StrategicDirection:
        theme_token: "GuidedFitConfidence"
        strategic_focus: >
          Position Marathon Sports HK as the approachable multi-brand decision point
          for everyday Hong Kong runners who want to stop guessing and choose with
          practical fitting clarity.
        audience_takeaway: >
          I do not need to guess which running shoe is right for me; Marathon Sports HK
          lets me test and compare brands with free fitting support before I buy.

      IdentityMigrationVector:
        departure_identity: >
          Unsure running-shoe guesser overwhelmed by brands, models, technologies,
          online advice, price risk, and uncertainty about whether fitting is for them.
        arrival_identity: >
          Confident everyday Hong Kong runner who has compared real options,
          understands their fit better, and feels ready to choose without
          single-brand pressure.
        migration_vector: "Reassurance"
        behavioral_shift: >
          From passive browsing and guessing to booking a free fitting or visiting
          Marathon Sports HK for guided multi-brand comparison.

    NarrativeInputs:
      NarrativeLensSelection:
        primary_lens:
          name: "Modern Freedom"
          role: "Remove the burden of decoding every running-shoe option alone."
        secondary_lens:
          name: "Quiet Confidence"
          role: "Make confidence calm, useful, and non-elite."
        supporting_lens:
          name: "Effortless Capability"
          role: "Make booking or store visit feel like an easy first step."
        emotional_intensity: "Medium"

      CurrentEmotionalState:
        primary_state: "Uncertain"
        supporting_states:
          - "Overwhelmed by brands, models, and technologies"
          - "Cautious about wasting money on the wrong pair"
          - "Unsure whether fitting is meant for casual runners"
          - "Suspicious of single-brand pressure"
          - "Stuck in passive browsing and comparison"
        emotional_truth: >
          The shopper wants to choose well, but does not feel personally equipped to decide.
        non_shaming_frame: >
          The uncertainty is normal, understandable, and solvable.

      PsychologicalFriction:
        primary_friction: "Choice overload plus purchase-risk anxiety"
        supporting_frictions:
          - "Too much generic online advice, not enough personal fit clarity"
          - "Fear of spending HK$1,000+ on the wrong shoe"
          - "Belief that fitting may be intimidating or athletes-only"
          - "Concern that one brand or one model will be pushed"
          - "Lack of a concrete reason to move from scrolling to store action"
        friction_interpretation: >
          The audience is not blocked by lack of interest. They are blocked by lack
          of decision confidence.

      DesiredTransformation:
        core_arc: "Confusion → supported clarity → confident choice"
        transformation_pairs:
          - "Brand guessing → guided multi-brand comparison"
          - "Purchase anxiety → free pre-purchase checkpoint"
          - "Fitting intimidation → approachable expert support"
          - "Passive browsing → booking or store visit"
          - "Generic running advice → guidance around own feet, stride, goals, and Hong Kong running context"
        transformation_limit: >
          Do not resolve the tension through injury-prevention claims, medical fear,
          or single-brand certainty.

      ViewerTakeaway:
        primary_takeaway: >
          I do not need to guess which running shoe is right for me; Marathon Sports HK
          lets me test and compare brands with free fitting support before I buy.
        remembered_phrase: "Stop guessing. Get guided. Choose with confidence."

  VisualIntent:
    PrimaryIntent: "Calm capability through guided choice"
    IntentDefinition: >
      The visual should make running-shoe fitting feel like a practical confidence
      step before purchase: calm, helpful, local, and easy to act on. The viewer
      should immediately feel that uncertainty has a clear next step, without
      being made to feel inexperienced or pressured.
    EmotionalObjective: >
      Replace the stress of too many running-shoe choices with the relief of
      being guided through real options.
    BehavioralObjective: >
      Make booking or visiting Marathon Sports HK feel like the sensible,
      low-pressure action after browsing.
    IntentQualities:
      - "Reassuring"
      - "Practical"
      - "Approachable"
      - "Everyday"
      - "Multi-brand"
      - "Low-pressure"
      - "Locally relevant"
      - "Confidence-building"
    MustNotFeelLike:
      - "A performance test"
      - "A medical assessment"
      - "A luxury fashion ritual"
      - "A single-brand endorsement"
      - "An elite athlete-only experience"
      - "A vague lifestyle mood piece"

  VisualConcept:
    ConceptName: "The Guided Fit Checkpoint"
    ConceptType: "Guided Experience"
    ConceptDefinition: >
      The image should psychologically express a moment where an everyday runner
      stops carrying the burden of choice alone and enters a supported comparison
      process. Marathon Sports HK should feel like the trusted checkpoint between
      social-feed confusion and confident purchase decision.
    ConceptualPremise: >
      Choosing running shoes is not about guessing harder; it is about getting
      guided, comparing properly, and deciding with more confidence before spending.
    CoreVisualIdea: >
      A practical, human, multi-brand decision moment where the shopper’s uncertainty
      is visibly converted into supported clarity.
    SymbolicVisualFraming:
      ChoiceOverloadSymbolism: >
        Many possible options should be conceptually present as legitimate choices,
        not as chaos or intimidation.
      GuidanceSymbolism: >
        Expert support should read as helpful partnership rather than authority,
        pressure, or sales dominance.
      ComparisonSymbolism: >
        Multiple brands should feel available for evaluation, with no one shoe or
        logo positioned as the universal answer.
      FreeFittingSymbolism: >
        The fitting service should feel like a smart pre-purchase checkpoint,
        reducing risk before commitment.
      HongKongRunningSymbolism: >
        The visual world should feel connected to everyday Hong Kong running life:
        practical, urban, local, familiar, and inclusive.
    ConceptualDo:
      - "Show the emotional shift from uncertainty to clarity."
      - "Make multi-brand comparison feel useful and empowering."
      - "Make expert help feel approachable for beginners and returning runners."
      - "Keep Marathon Sports HK as the owner of the fitting experience."
      - "Make the CTA feel like a practical next step, not a hard sell."
    ConceptualDont:
      - "Do not make one brand look like the answer."
      - "Do not frame the runner as elite, heroic, or medically at risk."
      - "Do not make the fitting feel technical, intimidating, or clinical."
      - "Do not reduce the campaign to generic shoe beauty imagery."
      - "Do not replace retail clarity with abstract athletic inspiration."

  SubjectRelationshipLogic:
    PrimaryRelationship: "Supported self-direction"
    RelationshipDefinition: >
      The shopper remains the decision-maker, while Marathon Sports HK provides
      expert guidance, context, and comparison support. The relationship should
      feel equal, respectful, and confidence-building.
    ViewerToSceneRelationship: >
      The viewer should feel invited into a helpful, normal, everyday retail action:
      “This is for someone like me, even if I am not an expert runner.”
    ShopperToExpertRelationship:
      dynamic: "Guidance without pressure"
      meaning: >
        The expert helps the shopper understand fit and options without overpowering
        their choice or pushing a predetermined answer.
    ShopperToBrandsRelationship:
      dynamic: "Comparison, not loyalty pressure"
      meaning: >
        Brands are available options to test and compare, not a hierarchy where one
        partner brand is presented as the winner.
    BrandToAudienceRelationship:
      dynamic: "Trusted local curator"
      meaning: >
        Marathon Sports HK reduces confusion by organizing choice around the shopper’s
        needs, not by demanding athletic identity or technical expertise.
    RelationshipProtections:
      - "Preserve shopper agency."
      - "Preserve expert warmth."
      - "Preserve multi-brand neutrality."
      - "Preserve beginner inclusion."
      - "Preserve low-pressure action."

    DesiredViewerInterpretations:
      - "I do not have to decode every shoe alone."
      - "This is a normal, practical step before buying."
      - "I can compare brands without being pushed into one."
      - "Free fitting lowers the risk of choosing wrong."
      - "This service is for everyday Hong Kong runners, not only serious athletes."
      - "I can get expert help and still make my own choice."
      - "Booking or visiting now makes practical sense."
    EmotionalResidue:
      - "Relief"
      - "Clarity"
      - "Support"
      - "Inclusion"
      - "Readiness"
      - "Quiet confidence"
    TransformationRead:
      before: "Too many options, too much risk, too much guessing."
      during: "A guided checkpoint that makes comparison manageable."
      after: "A better-informed shopper ready to choose with confidence."
    PerceptualRiskToAvoid:
      - "This looks like a hard athletic assessment."
      - "This looks like a brand promotion disguised as fitting."
      - "This looks like a medical solution."
      - "This looks too premium or exclusive."
      - "This looks inspirational but does not explain why to visit."

  AttentionControl:
    StructuralDensity: "Medium"
    DensityRationale: >
      The campaign needs enough perceptual richness to signal real choice, local

      feels the same overload the campaign is trying to solve.
    FocalPriority:
      primary: "The emotional shift from guessing to guided comparison"
      secondary: "Marathon Sports HK as the trusted multi-brand curator"
      tertiary: "Free fitting as the low-risk pre-purchase action"
    CognitiveLoadPrinciple: >

      compare brands, and choose with confidence. Any visual element that increases
      confusion without strengthening that idea should be removed.
    MessageHierarchy:
      first_read: "Help is available before you buy."
      second_read: "You can compare multiple brands in one trusted place."
      third_read: "This is approachable for everyday Hong Kong runners."
      action_read: "Book or visit for free fitting."
