# Purpose

Synthesis is the final creative reasoning layer.

Synthesis resolves all upstream decisions into a single authoritative Synthesis Contract.

Core Question:

Given all upstream decisions, what communication should the audience ultimately receive?

Synthesis may:

- consolidate
- resolve
- prioritize
- simplify
- suppress
- preserve

Synthesis may not:

- introduce new meaning
- introduce new entities
- introduce new relationships
- introduce new actions
- introduce new environments
- introduce new rendering styles

All creative decisions must originate upstream.

---

# Contract Resolution

Purpose:

Reduce downstream reasoning complexity by reconciling upstream contracts into a coherent decision space before communication decisions are made.

Output:

The Resolved Decision Space is consumed by all downstream resolution stages but is not included in the final Synthesis Contract.

# Observable Signal Mapping

Reduce interpretation ambiguity by converting communication into independently observable meaning components before visual translation.

Communication Resolution
↓

↓

## Communication Components

Purpose:

Break Communication Resolution into the core meanings that a viewer must perceive for the communication to succeed.

Communication Components should be expressed as concise meaning labels, typically one to three words.

Question: What distinct meanings must a viewer perceive?

A communication meaning must:
- represent a single meaning
- support the Communication Resolution
- be independently understandable
- be capable of being perceived through human behavior
- be capable of being translated into Human Signals
- avoid entities, environments, actions, composition, and rendering instructions

A communication meaning should disappear if its removal would weaken or change the intended communication.

## Human Signal

Must remain:

- perception-oriented
- communication aligned
- scene agnostic
- model agnostic

## Observable Requirements

Must remain:
- visually observable
- communication aligned
- scene agnostic
- model agnostic

## Visual Evidence Examples

Must remain:
- visually observable
- communication aligned
- scene agnostic
- model agnostic
- illustrative rather than prescriptive

Must not:
- define scene construction
- define composition
- define rendering behavior
- introduce new entities
- introduce new relationships
- introduce new actions

# Execution Resolution

Resolve how communication should be embodied within the experience.

Execution Resolution translates communication requirements into execution approaches that can later be realized through scene construction.

Question: How should communication become experience?

Execution Resolution must resolve:
- Primary Execution Mode
- Supporting Execution Modes
- Execution Requirements
- Excluded Execution Modes

Execution Resolution may not:
- construct scenes
- define composition
- define rendering behavior
- introduce new communication
- introduce new entities
- introduce new environments
- override preserved requirements

Execution Resolution defines embodiment strategy.

Scene Assembly determines how the strategy is realized.

---

## Primary Execution Mode

Resolve the dominant embodiment strategy.

Question: How should the Primary Communication be experienced?

Only one Primary Execution Mode should exist.

---

## Supporting Execution Modes

Resolve supporting embodiment strategies.

Question: How should Secondary Communication be reinforced?

Supporting Execution Modes should remain subordinate to the Primary Execution Mode.

---

## Execution Requirements

Resolve the minimum execution conditions required for the embodiment strategy to remain possible.

Question: What execution conditions are required for the execution strategy to remain viable?

Execution Requirements should:
- remain communication aligned
- remain scene agnostic
- remain rendering agnostic
- describe categories rather than implementations

---

## Excluded Execution Modes

Resolve embodiment strategies that would undermine communication.

Question: How must communication not be embodied?

Execution Rules
- execution must preserve communication
- execution must remain observable
- execution must remain scene agnostic
- execution must remain rendering agnostic
- primary execution mode takes priority
- execution requirements support execution modes
- excluded execution modes override all other execution modes

# Rendering Resolution

Resolve the visual expression behaviors that must survive downstream translation.

Question: What visual expression truths must survive?

Rendering Resolution must resolve:

- Perceptual Rendering
- Physical Rendering
- Rendering Quality
- Authenticity Behavior

Rendering Resolution may not:

- introduce new rendering styles
- introduce new visual language
- override Composition & Rendering decisions
- reinterpret communication

All rendering decisions must originate upstream.


## Rendering Resolution Rules

Rendering outputs should resolve observable rendering behaviors, NOT abstract quality ratings.

Outputs should remain directly translatable into prompt language without requiring additional interpretation.

Avoid:
- High
- Medium
- Low
- Required
- Optional

Prefer:
- Descriptions of desired visual behavior
- Descriptions of material behavior
- Descriptions of human behavior
- Descriptions of lighting behavior
- Descriptions of authenticity behavior

---

## Perceptual Rendering

Resolve how the communication should be emotionally interpreted by the viewer.

Output:
- Emotional Tone
- Atmosphere

---

## Physical Rendering

Resolve how visual expression should behave physically.

Output:
- Reality Model
- Camera Intent
- Camera Specs
- Lighting Intent
- Luminance Hierarchy
- Material Behavior
- Optical Intent

Camera Specs rule: `focal_length_mm` and `aperture_f_stop` are copied **verbatim** from the CompositionRenderingContract's `CameraPlan` — never paraphrased, rounded, or absorbed into prose. They are mechanical constants the Prompt Compiler must receive as values, not descriptions. `CameraIntent` prose covers perspective, framing, and viewer relationship; it must not restate the numbers.

Luminance Hierarchy rule: `luminance_priority` values are copied **verbatim per entity** from the CompositionRenderingContract's `HierarchyPlan` into `LuminanceHierarchy` — a list of `{element, luminance_priority}` pairs, not prose. `LightingIntent` describes how brightness is achieved (light sources, exposure behavior) and must remain consistent with these rankings, but must not restate them as a ranked list itself.

---

## Rendering Quality

Resolve the technical image quality characteristics required for execution.

Output:
- Detail Rendering
- Texture Rendering
- Lighting Rendering
- Depth Rendering
- Commercial Rendering

---

## Authenticity Behavior

Resolve how authenticity should be preserved through rendering.

Output:
- Human Authenticity Rendering
- Environmental Authenticity Rendering
- Material Authenticity Rendering
- Imperfection Rendering

Rules:
- authenticity must support communication
- variation must support credibility
- realism must not become idealization
- authenticity takes priority over perfection
- human believability takes priority over visual spectacle
- rendering must reinforce participation before performance

# Preservation Resolution

Resolve the information that must survive all downstream interpretation and transformation.

Question: What information must not be changed?

Preservation Resolution protects information that cannot be safely abstracted, generalized, or substituted.

Preservation Resolution may include:
- client requirements
- contractual requirements
- brand requirements
- product requirements
- location requirements
- people requirements
- legal requirements
- narrative requirements

Preservation Resolution overrides all downstream interpretation.

If a preserved requirement conflicts with a downstream decision, the preserved requirement takes priority.

Output:
- **Preserved Entities:** Specific entities that must survive unchanged.
- **Preserved Context:** Specific contextual requirements that must survive unchanged.
- **Preserved Brand Requirements:** Specific brand requirements that must survive unchanged.
- **Preserved Narrative Requirements:** Specific narrative requirements that must survive unchanged.
- **Preserved Constraints:** Any additional non-negotiable requirements.

## Preservation Rules

- preserved information may not be abstracted
- preserved information may not be substituted
- preserved information may not be removed
- preserved information overrides downstream interpretation
